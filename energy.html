About ORIX Energy

1,000+ sites of trusted solar execution

ORIX Energy has established itself as one of the experienced and trusted names in the solar sector — backed by a professional, dedicated technical team, strong financial stability, and the operational capacity to execute projects of varying scale.

With offices and operational presence including the KP Industrial Estate, Peshawar, Hayatabad, and other locations, ORIX Energy has been among the pioneers of the solar market in the region — contributing to the transition toward cleaner, more reliable, and sustainable energy.

Who we are

A group built on management, stability, and quality

ORIX Group of Companies operates across three key sectors — Energy, Agriculture, and Hospitality — combining professional expertise, strong management, and financial stability with a commitment to long-term growth. Each business runs independently, guided by the same standard: build things that last.

From solar sites lighting up Khyber Pakhtunkhwa to livestock and crops rising from the soil of Risalpur, to a chalet retreat cooled by the pines of Changla Gali — the group's work spans the essentials of modern life: power, food, and rest.

About Cedar Lodges

A retreat beyond ordinary accommodation

Surrounded by the natural beauty of the mountains, forests, and cool alpine environment, Cedar Lodges & Residences offers an exclusive and memorable mountain retreat for guests seeking comfort, privacy, nature, and tranquility.

Whether it's a family getaway, a romantic escape, a peaceful holiday, or simply a break from the busy city life — Cedar Lodges & Residences offers a distinctive mountain experience where nature, luxury, comfort, and hospitality come together.
